ASHEN COVENANT: THE BLACK ROAD 6.0.0

PLAY
1. Extract the complete ZIP to a normal folder.
2. Keep all files and the resources folder together.
3. Double-click "Ashen Covenant.exe".

Windows SmartScreen may warn because this independent build is unsigned. Use
"More info" and "Run anyway" only when you trust the source of this archive.

CONTROLS
- Move: WASD, left stick, or left-click ground
- Select/approach/one committed attack: left-click a creature
- Attack: J or controller A
- Primary skills: Q / E or controller X / Y
- Dodge: Space or controller B
- Companion Technique: C or controller LB
- Hybrid Signature: F or controller RB
- Ultimate: R or controller RT
- Flask: G or controller LT
- Interact: X or left-stick press
- Covenant Hub: Escape or controller Menu

THE BLACK ROAD
- Sanctuary stays clear. Open the Atlas and deliberately launch an expedition.
- Five routes contain 20 sealed rooms: formation, ritual, lieutenant, and boss.
- Ritual vessels protect their formation until you destroy all three.
- Midpoint route choices alter Heat, rewards, boons, and banes.
- Boss arenas shrink and change through multiple combat phases.
- Animated enemy motion sheets, fixed-facing 2.5D heroes, painted terrain,
  entrances, props, and NPCs replace the old proxy geometry. Actor art has no
  geometric fallback.
- Players and enemies use grounded elevation and gravity. Character bodies
  step through authored facing stances rather than freely spinning 360 degrees.
- Every basic attack, spell, Companion Technique, hybrid action, ultimate, and
  enemy wind-up plays an authored 8-frame generated VFX sequence synchronized
  to its combat timing.
- Every primary class has a visible build-and-spend mechanic.
- Adventurer, Veteran, and Penitent difficulties remain available in Settings.
- Prior covenants migrate automatically to save version 19 with Covenant, Hunter,
  world-state, Sanctuary, and Black Road records initialized safely.

The full release report and test evidence are in docs/ in the source archive.
